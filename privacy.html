Privacy policy

This policy describes the BookGuard website and the BookGuard skills-based ChatGPT plugin, published by Derek Dardis.

Website data

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not contain a BookGuard sign-in form, newsletter, advertising tracker, or analytics script. We do not ask visitors to submit personal information on this site. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under its own policies.

Using the ChatGPT plugin

BookGuard runs through ChatGPT and uses capabilities available in that service. The publisher does not operate a separate BookGuard backend for receiving or storing book-screening conversations. ChatGPT and any research sources it accesses may process information according to their own policies and your account settings. Avoid sharing unnecessary personal or sensitive information, especially about children.

Support requests

Support is provided through public GitHub Issues. Information you choose to post in an issue may be publicly visible and processed by GitHub. Please do not post personal information about children or other sensitive details. You can manage your own issues through GitHub, subject to GitHub’s policies.

Data sales and payments

BookGuard does not operate a data-sales program or payment checkout through this website or plugin. Use of ChatGPT may be subject to the platform’s own terms and plan limits.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as the project evolves. Updated wording will be posted here. For privacy questions, use BookGuard support, and avoid sharing private details in a public issue.
