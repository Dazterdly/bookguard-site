For questions, feature requests, inaccurate content warnings, or bugs, please open a GitHub issue. This lets us respond without requiring a public personal email address.

Protect your privacy

Do not include a child’s full name, contact details, medical information, private conversation screenshots, or other sensitive information in a public issue. GitHub issues in this public repository are visible to everyone.
